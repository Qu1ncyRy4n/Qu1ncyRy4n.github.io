## Interests

- Visual attention and meaning guidance during complex scene viewing; eye-tracking methods.
- Computational and neural mechanisms of scene attention and comprehension.
- Social cognition, theory of mind, and individual differences in social attention.
- Assistive and translational applications of attention research.
